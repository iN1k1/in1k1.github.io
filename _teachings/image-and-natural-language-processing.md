**Degree programme:** Bachelor's in Digital Technologies and Communication for Creative Industries (TED), Digital Imaging track. 9 CFU, taught in Italian. Attendance is not mandatory.

**Teaching methods:** lectures plus laboratory activities where students work on real data under the lecturer's guidance.

**Assessment:** a written exam with exercises and questions on the topics covered in class. Slides and lab material are provided by the lecturer.

Coding resources: [image-and-natural-language-processing-uniud](https://github.com/iN1k1/image-and-natural-language-processing-uniud).
