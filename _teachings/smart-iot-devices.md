**Degree programme:** Master's in Multimedia Communication and Information Technologies (Artificial Intelligence, Industrial Automation and IoT track). 9 CFU, taught in English. Attendance is not mandatory.

**Teaching methods:** lectures plus laboratory activities where students develop projects on IoT platforms under the lecturer's guidance.

**Assessment:** oral presentation of an individual (or two-person) project agreed with the lecturer, plus a short written report submitted at least 5 days before the exam.

Coding resources: [smart-iot-devices-uniud](https://github.com/iN1k1/smart-iot-devices-uniud).
